    The process

    Three steps, in order — because this is the order it actually happens in.

    01

    Tell us about yourself

    Education stage, category, marks, income, state — the same fields every scholarship form asks for anyway.

    02

    We match you instantly

    Every scholarship is checked against your exact numbers, not just your keywords. No scrolling through 40 that reject you at step 3.

    03

    Follow the roadmap

    Each match comes with the documents to arrange and the exact next step — income certificate, bonafide letter, bank passbook copy, and so on.
